Menu Policy:

1. Menu items must have a name, category, price, and availability status.
2. A menu item can be marked as veg or non-veg.
3. A menu item can be marked as spicy or non-spicy.
4. Admin can add, edit, delete, search, filter, and paginate menu items.
5. If an item is unavailable, customers should not be able to order it.
6. Featured items can be shown on the dashboard or homepage.
7. Image upload is optional but recommended for real restaurant use.
