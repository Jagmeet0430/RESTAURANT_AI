Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: What is RestaurantAI?
A: RestaurantAI is a restaurant management platform with AI features.

Q: What AI features are included?
A: Sales prediction, OCR menu digitization, chatbot, and voice recommendation.

Q: Can the admin manage menu items?
A: Yes, the admin can add, edit, delete, search, filter, and update menu items.

Q: Can the system predict sales?
A: Yes, the sales prediction module predicts future sales using historical data.

Q: Can this project be used by a real restaurant?
A: Yes, after proper testing, deployment, security setup, and database verification.
